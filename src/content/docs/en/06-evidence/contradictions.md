---
title: Contradictory Evidence
description: Register of conflicting or non-replicating evidence.
---

No contradictory evidence has been reviewed yet.

For each conflict, document:

- Claim and evidence items on each side.
- Differences in population, system, method, measurement, date, and context.
- Whether the conflict is substantive, methodological, or only apparent.
- Replication or adjudication plan, if any.
- Implications for evidence level and related guideline wording.

Do not silently discard results that fail to support a preferred recommendation.
