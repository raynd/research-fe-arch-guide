## Current limitations

- No primary literature has been appraised and no experiments have been executed.
- The target crawlers, retrieval systems, and tools have not been selected.
- Proposed metrics and controls have not been piloted.
- External answer systems may be opaque, dynamic, personalized, and non-reproducible.
- Results from a controlled/local pipeline may not generalize to other systems.

Update this page as limitations are discovered. Each finding and guideline should also state its own specific boundaries.
