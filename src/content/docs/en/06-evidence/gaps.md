This register is preliminary; no literature review has yet been completed.

## Initial gaps to assess

- Whether frontend-only changes have measurable effects across independent extraction and retrieval pipelines.
- How source DOM structure survives or is transformed by real parsing and chunking stages.
- Whether structured data provides incremental value when facts are visible and equivalent.
- How rendering behavior varies by crawler and test date.
- How to separate retrieval success from generated answer quality and citation support.
- How transferable local or controlled results are to opaque production systems.

Reclassify these as confirmed gaps only after documenting the search and screening process. Link each gap to relevant questions and literature review coverage.
