## Traceability rules

- Link verified paper IDs or experiment/run IDs; include source locations where useful.
- Separate published evidence, project experiment results, and observational notes; do not merge them into one row.
- Describe what the evidence actually measured, not a broader implication.
- Justify each level using quality, independence, replication, and applicability.
- Mark conflicting results rather than averaging them away.
- Link every guideline to supporting findings and evidence rows; if no evidence exists, label it a hypothesis or leave it unapproved.
