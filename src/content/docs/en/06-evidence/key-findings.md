## Finding record template

For each new `FIND-###`, record:

- Concise claim and the population/system/context it applies to.
- Evidence level and justification.
- Source IDs, experiment/run IDs, dates, and relevant data locations.
- Methodological quality, uncertainty, limitations, and counterevidence.
- Whether the claim is replicated and by whom.
- Related hypotheses, research questions, and `FE-###` guideline IDs.

Separate a source's published conclusion from this project's synthesis. Update the [evidence matrix](/en/06-evidence/matrix/) in the same change.
