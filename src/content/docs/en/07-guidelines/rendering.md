## Topics

SSR, SSG, CSR, initial HTML, hydration, JavaScript dependency, crawler accessibility.

## Candidate questions

- Is required content present in the initial response?
- Does a declared client retrieve or render the relevant content?
- What are the trade-offs in freshness, complexity, cost, and user experience?

No guideline is approved in this area. Compare any proposed recommendation with [EXP-002](/en/05-experiments/exp-002/) and record findings before assigning a priority.
