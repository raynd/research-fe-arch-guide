## Status

This is the planned final deliverable. No architecture guidelines have been approved because no literature review or experiments have been completed. Topic pages below define areas to investigate, not recommendations.

## Evidence policy

Every eventual recommendation must link to specific findings, study or experiment records, an evidence level and justification, validation method, limitations, and relevant exceptions. Keep scientific support distinct from project observations and plausible design reasoning.

Priorities are **Must**, **Should**, and **Context-dependent**. Assign them only after evaluating evidence, costs, risks, and applicability.
