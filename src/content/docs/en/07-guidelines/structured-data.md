## Topics

Schema.org, JSON-LD, entity relationships, structured metadata, and consistency between visible content and structured data.

## Candidate questions

- Is syntax validity distinguished from demonstrated downstream use?
- Does structured data add measurable value when equivalent visible content exists?
- How are discrepancies, stale values, and unsupported relationships handled?
