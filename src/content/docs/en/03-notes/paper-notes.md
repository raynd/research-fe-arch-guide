No paper notes have been added. Create one note per verified source using this template:

```markdown
---
title: "Verified paper title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
paperId: PAPER-###
---

## Bibliographic record
- Authors:
- Year:
- Venue:
- DOI or stable URL:
- Publication status:

## Research topic and question
## Methodology and sample
## Main findings (as stated by the authors)
## Limitations (as stated and independently noted)
## Relevance to this research
## Claims cited and page/section
## Appraisal and follow-up questions
```

Do not treat an abstract as a substitute for assessing methods and limitations when the full source is available.
