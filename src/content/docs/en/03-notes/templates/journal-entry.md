---
title: Research Journal Entry Template
description: Reusable Markdown template for a chronological research journal entry.
---

Copy this template to `research-journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and replace the prompts. Keep uncertainty explicit.

```markdown
---
title: YYYY-MM-DD — Short activity title
date: YYYY-MM-DD
---

## Research activity
## What I investigated
## What I learned
## Important observations
## Changed assumptions
## New questions
## Next steps
## Related sources and research objects
```
