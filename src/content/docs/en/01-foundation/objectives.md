---
title: Research Objectives
description: Research objectives guiding the investigation.
---

1. Investigate how semantic HTML relates to extraction and interpretation compared with generic DOM structures.
2. Compare CSR, SSR, and SSG with respect to meaningful content availability and observed crawler or retrieval behavior.
3. Examine whether structured data adds measurable entity or answer value when equivalent facts are visible in HTML.
4. Study how source content organization relates to downstream parsing, segmentation, chunking, and retrieval.
5. Derive technology-independent architecture recommendations only where supported, and state evidence gaps and trade-offs.

These are objectives, not conclusions. See [research questions](/en/01-foundation/questions/) and the [proposed experiments](/en/05-experiments/).
