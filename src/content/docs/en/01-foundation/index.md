## Purpose

This project studies how frontend implementation choices may affect how web content is rendered, parsed, retrieved, interpreted, and cited by generative search and answer systems.

The intended deliverable is a set of technology-independent guidelines whose recommendations are traceable to sources, experiment results, and explicit evidence assessments.

## Research lifecycle

1. Establish the research problem, questions, scope, and method.
2. Review and record literature without inventing or extrapolating findings.
3. Maintain hypotheses and proposed experiments as explicitly provisional.
4. Record results with enough detail for reproduction and critical review.
5. Synthesize evidence into guidelines with limitations and validation methods.

## Evidence discipline

Use the [evidence levels](/en/06-evidence/) only when justified by actual evidence. Distinguish published claims from this project's observations and recommendations. No findings or guideline claims have yet been established.
