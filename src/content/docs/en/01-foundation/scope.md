---
title: Scope & Limitations
description: What this research includes, excludes, and cannot infer by default.
---

## In scope

- Frontend document structure, semantics, rendering, visible content, metadata, accessibility, and technical quality.
- Observable content extraction, retrieval, answer, and citation outcomes when measurement is feasible.
- Comparisons that isolate frontend variables and document the evaluated systems and dates.
- Evidence-based guidance that remains technology-independent where possible.

## Out of scope unless explicitly added

- Claims that a specific search or answer system uses a particular undisclosed ranking or retrieval algorithm.
- Generalized ranking guarantees or assurances of inclusion, citation, or traffic.
- Backend, editorial, or off-page interventions except as controlled factors or explicit limitations.
- Treating accessibility conformance, HTML validity, or structured-data validation as proof of GEO/AEO impact.

## Limitations to track

External systems may be opaque, change over time, personalize results, or differ across crawlers. Small test corpora may not generalize. Extraction and answer metrics capture different stages. Observational correlations do not establish causal effects. Each study must declare its sampling, environment, dates, and uncertainty.
