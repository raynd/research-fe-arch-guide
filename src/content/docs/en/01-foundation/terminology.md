title: Terminology & Definitions
description: Working definitions to keep research claims precise and consistent.
---

These are working definitions for this project, not claims about the internal design of any particular provider.

For technical definitions and related concepts, use the central [Glossary / Quick Knowledge](/en/13-reference/glossary/). This page retains the project's measurement and evidence vocabulary.

| Term              | Working definition                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Answerability     | The degree to which available content supports a response to a specified question, as operationalized by a study. |
| Citation accuracy | Whether a citation identifies a source that supports the attributed claim, according to a declared rubric.        |
| Evidence          | A traceable source or observation relevant to a claim; strength depends on methods and applicability.             |

Revise definitions if literature uses terms differently; preserve the source-specific meaning when reporting a study.
