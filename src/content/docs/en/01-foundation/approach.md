title: Research Approach
description: A staged and iterative approach from literature to architecture guidance.
---

## Stages

1. **Frame:** freeze question versions, definitions, review criteria, causal model, and intended inference.
2. **Review:** run and log searches, screen sources, appraise methods, and extract evidence.
3. **Design:** operationalize hypotheses, treatment contrasts, outcomes, units, controls, and an analysis plan.
4. **Pilot:** test fixture parity, measurement validity, variance, run failures, annotation guidance, and feasibility.
5. **Execute:** run the versioned confirmatory protocol; preserve all runs and protocol deviations.
6. **Analyze:** evaluate the predeclared outcomes first; report exploratory analyses separately.
7. **Synthesize:** assess evidence quality and applicability, resolve neither contradictions nor nulls by vote counting, and draft traceable guidelines.

## Stage gates

- **Review-ready:** search strategy, eligibility criteria, source log, and screening process versioned.
- **Experiment-ready:** estimand, primary outcome, comparator, unit, dataset, controls, and analysis plan frozen; content parity and access requirements checked.
- **Evidence-ready:** raw outputs, exclusions, deviations, uncertainty, and limitations available for independent review.
- **Guideline-ready:** recommendation is scoped to the evidence, traceable to findings, tested for trade-offs, and has a measurable validation method.

Failure at a gate means revise or label the work exploratory; it does not mean discard inconvenient observations. Stages may iterate. Journal changes to questions or protocols and state their impact on comparability.

## Research object conventions

Preserve existing IDs (`H-001`–`H-006`, `EXP-001`–`EXP-004`). Use `PAPER-###`, `HYP-###`, `EXP-###`, `FIND-###`, and `FE-###` for new records, and link related objects. Keep statuses explicit. A missing result is not a negative result; an untested hypothesis is not supported or rejected.

Keep stable IDs separate from language: both locale records share the same identifier and relative page path. Record claims at the narrowest level supported (study, measured system, outcome stage, and date), not as unqualified statements about “AI systems.”
