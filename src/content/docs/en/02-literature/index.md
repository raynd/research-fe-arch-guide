## Source review checklist

- Verify title, author list, year, venue, DOI/URL, and publication status from a reliable source.
- Record search query, database or venue, date searched, and screening rationale.
- Separate the source's claims from this project's interpretation.
- Capture methods and limitations before judging relevance or evidence quality.
- Include null or contradictory results; do not select sources only because they support an expected guideline.
